The bundled JPEGs are resized copies of the source photos; the interface also crops them to fit thumbnail frames. The resized Beyaynetu and Kitfo assets are distributed under their corresponding CC BY-SA licenses. The photos are representative examples, not photographs of a particular restaurant's own plates. Restaurant owners can replace a sample photo or upload their own exact dish photo from the menu editor. User-uploaded photos stay on the device and are not generated or sent to a server.
